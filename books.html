Book suggestions

Start with the Bible. These help you stay with it.

This is a short shelf for people in our English-speaking congregation — not an official exam list. If you want a recommendation for your season, ask the pastor.

Holy Bible

The Holy Bible

Read a Gospel first if you are new — Mark or John. Bring a paper Bible on Sunday if you have one. English translations such as NIV, ESV, or KJV are all welcome in the room.

Lewis

Mere Christianity — C. S. Lewis

Clear help for people with questions. Good if you believe, and good if you are not sure yet.

Tozer

The Pursuit of God — A. W. Tozer

Short, serious, and useful when you want God Himself, not only church activity.

Packer

Knowing God — J. I. Packer

For believers who want to grow past slogans into a sturdier knowledge of the Lord.

Bunyan

The Pilgrim’s Progress — John Bunyan

An old story about walking with Christ through a real life. Public domain, easy to find free.

Chambers

My Utmost for His Highest — Oswald Chambers

A daily companion if you like a short reading beside the verse on our Daily Word page.

Nee

The Normal Christian Life — Watchman Nee

On life in Christ, not only effort. Helpful after you have been around church for a while.

Bonhoeffer

The Cost of Discipleship — Dietrich Bonhoeffer

A harder book, and a honest one, about following Jesus when it costs something.
