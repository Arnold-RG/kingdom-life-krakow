If you already believe

A church home in English.

If you are an English-speaking believer looking for a community in Kraków, come on Sunday. Stay after the service. Someone will want to meet you.

If you still have questions

You do not need to have it all settled.

If you are not a believer, but you have questions and want to learn about God, you are welcome. Bring the questions. We would rather talk than pretend.

Arriving

Your first Sunday.

  1. The building

    Hotel Centrum, Osiedle Centrum E 12. The service is inside the hotel, not in a church on the street.

  2. The signs

    Look for Kingdom Life posters and banners. They lead you to the room.

  3. The service

    From 12:00 to 14:00, in English: worship, teaching, and time with one another.

  4. Afterwards

    First-time guests are welcomed after the service. Stay if you can.

Questions people ask before they come.

Is there an English church in Kraków?

Yes. Kingdom Life Kraków holds English Sunday worship at Hotel Centrum, Osiedle Centrum E 12, every Sunday from 12:00 to 14:00.

Where does the church meet?

Inside Hotel Centrum, Osiedle Centrum E 12, 31-934 Kraków. Look for Kingdom Life posters and banners in the hotel. They lead to the room.

What time is Sunday, and what about prayer?

Sunday worship is from 12:00 to 14:00. The church also gathers for prayer at 18:00 through the week.

Is this an international church?

Yes. The room is English-speaking and international. Believers and people who are still asking questions are both welcome.

How do I join the WhatsApp group or membership?

Use the join page. The campus sends the group link. It is not posted on the open web. Public pages are on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and X.
