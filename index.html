Church facts

Społeczność Chrześcijańska Kingdom Life

Official English name: Kingdom Life Christian Fellowship. Google lists the Krakow campus as a Church of Christ. The family meets in Warsaw and Kraków. Tagline: Embracing God’s Love and Living His Light.

Vision: Know God, find purpose, and be empowered to live God’s life.
Mission: To raise kingdom-minded, community-relevant people.

Krakow campus: Sunday 12:00–14:00 inside Hotel Centrum, Osiedle Centrum E 12, 31-934 Kraków. Daily prayer at 18:00. Online Sunday services on YouTube.

Questions people type into Google

Is there an English church in Krakow?

Yes. Kingdom Life Kraków holds English Sunday worship at Hotel Centrum, Osiedle Centrum E 12, every Sunday from 12:00 to 14:00.

Is this an international church?

Yes. The room is English-speaking and international. Believers and seekers are both welcome.

How do I join the WhatsApp group or membership?

Use the join page to request the membership and WhatsApp community. Official public pages are on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and X.
