About the church

Kingdom Life Kraków

This page collects only what the church publishes about itself. Polish registered name: Społeczność Chrześcijańska Kingdom Life. English name: Kingdom Life Christian Fellowship. Google Maps category for the Krakow campus: Church of Christ.

What the church says it is

A diverse family united by love for Jesus and inspired by God’s Kingdom. They aim to raise kingdom-oriented, community-relevant believers in Warsaw and beyond through teaching and evangelism. Tagline: Embracing God’s Love and Living His Light.

Vision: Know God, find purpose, and be empowered to live God’s life.

Mission: To raise kingdom-minded, community-relevant people.

From the lead pastor: “Everybody is somebody and Jesus Christ is Lord.” First-time guests are welcomed after the service.
